> *Transformers process all tokens simultaneously, making them blind to word order without positional signals. While Absolute Sinusoidal PE directly adds coordinate vectors to input embeddings, **Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE)** injects position geometrically via 2D rotation matrices—preserving relative distance invariant to absolute sequence length.*

### The Core Puzzle: Absolute vs. Relative Distance

Human language relies primarily on **relative distance**, not absolute coordinates. In the sentence:

$$
\text{"The"} \quad \text{"cat"} \quad \text{"sat"} \quad \text{"on"} \quad \text{"the"} \quad \text{"mat"}
$$

The word `"cat"` precedes `"sat"` by exactly 1 token ($\Delta = -1$), whether this phrase begins at token position $0$ or token position $100{,}000$.

#### Why Absolute PE Fails at Scale

The original Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017) added an absolute position vector $\mathbf{p}_m$ directly to the token embedding $\mathbf{x}_m$:

$$
\mathbf{h}_m = \mathbf{x}_m + \mathbf{p}_m, \qquad \mathbf{p}_m^{(i)} = \begin{bmatrix} \sin(m \omega_i) \\ \cos(m \omega_i) \end{bmatrix}, \quad \omega_i = 10000^{-2i/d}
$$

While ingenious, this formulation suffers from three major flaws:

1. **Semantic Vector Distortion**: Adding $\mathbf{p}_m$ directly to $\mathbf{x}_m$ pollutes the learned word representations, forcing semantic features and positional signals to fight for bandwidth in the same vector space.
2. **Four Noisy Cross-Terms**: Taking the dot product of two embedded tokens $(\mathbf{x}_m + \mathbf{p}_m)^\top (\mathbf{x}_n + \mathbf{p}_n)$ unpacks into four terms:
   $$
   \underbrace{\mathbf{x}_m^\top \mathbf{x}_n}_{\text{Content-Content}} + \underbrace{\mathbf{x}_m^\top \mathbf{p}_n + \mathbf{p}_m^\top \mathbf{x}_n}_{\text{Content-Position Cross Terms}} + \underbrace{\mathbf{p}_m^\top \mathbf{p}_n}_{\text{Position-Position}}
   $$
   The mixed cross-terms introduce unpredictable noise and cannot guarantee monotonic distance decay.
3. **The Out-of-Distribution Ceiling**: If trained on sequences of length $L \le 4096$, evaluating at $m > 4096$ feeds unseen positional coordinates into the network, leading to catastrophic degradation.

**The Desired Solution**: Can we transform Query $\mathbf{q}_m$ and Key $\mathbf{k}_n$ such that their inner product depends **strictly on the token contents and relative distance $(n - m)$**, without altering vector norms?

<br/>

### The Minimal Math: 2D Complex Rotation

Su et al. (2021) formulated the RoPE condition: find transformations $f_q(\mathbf{x}_m, m)$ and $f_k(\mathbf{x}_n, n)$ that satisfy:

$$
\langle f_q(\mathbf{x}_m, m),\, f_k(\mathbf{x}_n, n) \rangle = g(\mathbf{x}_m, \mathbf{x}_n,\, n - m)
$$

By treating 2D vector coordinates as complex numbers, Euler's formula provides the unique non-trivial solution:

$$
\mathbf{q}_m \in \mathbb{C} \implies \mathbf{q}'_m = \mathbf{q}_m e^{i m \theta}
$$
$$
\mathbf{k}_n \in \mathbb{C} \implies \mathbf{k}'_n = \mathbf{k}_n e^{i n \theta}
$$

Taking the Hermitian inner product in complex space:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle \mathbf{q}'_m, \mathbf{k}'_n \rangle &= \operatorname{Re}\left[ \mathbf{q}'_m (\mathbf{k}'_n)^* \right] \\
&= \operatorname{Re}\left[ (\mathbf{q}_m e^{i m \theta}) (\mathbf{k}_n e^{-i n \theta}) \right] \\
&= \operatorname{Re}\left[ \mathbf{q}_m \mathbf{k}_n^* \, e^{-i (n - m)\theta} \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Real Matrix Equivalent

In standard real arithmetic, rotating a 2D vector by angle $m\theta$ corresponds to multiplying by the 2D rotation matrix $R(m\theta)$:

$$
R(m\theta) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(m\theta) & -\sin(m\theta) \\ \sin(m\theta) & \cos(m\theta) \end{bmatrix}
$$

Because rotation matrices are orthogonal ($R(\theta)^\top = R(-\theta)$ and $R(\theta_1) R(\theta_2) = R(\theta_1 + \theta_2)$):

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathbf{q}'_m)^\top \mathbf{k}'_n &= (R(m\theta)\mathbf{q}_m)^\top (R(n\theta)\mathbf{k}_n) \\
&= \mathbf{q}_m^\top R(m\theta)^\top R(n\theta) \mathbf{k}_n \\
&= \mathbf{q}_m^\top R(-m\theta) R(n\theta) \mathbf{k}_n \\
&= \mathbf{q}_m^\top R((n - m)\theta) \mathbf{k}_n
\end{aligned}
$$

**The Result**: Absolute positions $m$ and $n$ completely vanish from the interaction! The attention score depends purely on the content vectors modulated by the **relative rotation angle $(n - m)\theta$**, while preserving vector norms ($\|\mathbf{q}'_m\| = \|\mathbf{q}_m\|$).

<br/>

### Multi-Dimensional Embedding & Context Extrapolation

A single attention head has dimension $d_h$ (typically $64$ or $128$). To apply 2D rotations to high-dimensional representations, RoPE slices the vector into $d_h / 2$ orthogonal 2-dimensional planes:

$$
\mathcal{R}_m = \begin{bmatrix}
R(m\theta_0) & 0 & \dots & 0 \\
0 & R(m\theta_1) & \dots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & \dots & R(m\theta_{d_h/2 - 1})
\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \theta_i = 10000^{-2i / d_h}
$$

$$
\mathbf{q}'_m = \mathcal{R}_m \mathbf{q}_m, \qquad \mathbf{k}'_n = \mathcal{R}_n \mathbf{k}_n
$$

#### 1) Natural Distance Decay via Multi-Frequency Interference

The total attention inner product is the sum across all $d_h / 2$ subspaces:

$$
(\mathbf{q}'_m)^\top \mathbf{k}'_n = \sum_{i=0}^{d_h/2 - 1} \left( \mathbf{q}_m^{(i)} \right)^\top R((n - m)\theta_i) \, \mathbf{k}_n^{(i)}
$$

Because each subspace rotates at an exponentially different frequency $\theta_i$, summing across them induces **destructive interference** as distance $|n - m|$ grows large. This creates an automatic mathematical decay: tokens that are thousands of positions apart will naturally have lower attention weights unless their content vectors are overwhelmingly aligned.

#### 2) Extending Context Windows (NTK-Aware & YaRN)

RoPE's geometric formulation provides a clean mathematical lever for context length extension:

- **Position Interpolation (PI)**: Scaling positions by $m \to m / s$ (where $s$ is the expansion factor) allows the model to process $s \times$ longer contexts, but compresses high frequencies too aggressively, harming local syntactic precision.
- **NTK-Aware Scaling**: Rather than scaling positions $m$, adjust base wavelength $b = 10000 \to 10000 \cdot s^{d_h / (d_h - 2)}$. This leaves high-frequency dimensions mostly untouched to preserve local reasoning while stretching low-frequency dimensions to extend the context window.
- **YaRN (Yet another RoPE extensioN)**: Dispatches frequencies into three bands (untouched, interpolated, and ramped), allowing models like Llama 3 and DeepSeek to scale from 4K tokens up to 128K+ without catastrophic forgetting.

### Comparison & References

| Dimension | Absolute Sinusoidal PE | Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage Applied** | Input token embedding layer | Self-Attention Query / Key projections |
| **Target Vector** | Entire token embedding $\mathbf{x}_m \in \mathbb{R}^d$ | Per-head Query $\mathbf{q}_m$, Key $\mathbf{k}_n \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$ |
| **Mathematical Operation** | Elementwise addition ($\mathbf{x}_m + \mathbf{p}_m$) | Orthogonal 2D rotation ($\mathcal{R}_m \mathbf{q}_m$) |
| **Norm Preservation** | Distorts vector norms & shifts semantic center | Strict isometry: $\|\mathcal{R}_m \mathbf{q}\| = \|\mathbf{q}\|$ |
| **Relative Distance** | Imprecise; corrupted by cross-terms | Mathematically exact: $\mathbf{q}_m^\top R((n-m)\theta)\mathbf{k}_n$ |
| **Context Extrapolation** | Brittle; fails outside training length | Readily extrapolatable via NTK / YaRN scaling |
